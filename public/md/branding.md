# Brand Identity & Business Setup for Ecommerce

TMMT handles the compliance and setup work most founders don't want to deal with — GST filings, trademark applications, FBA/FBF registrations, and marketplace onboarding. So the brand can actually ship.

## Four-Phase Process

### Phase 01: Statutory Entity
- HSN/SAC mapping
- GST nexus filing
- Trademark (TM) procurement

### Phase 02: Commercial Setup
- Current account / treasury setup
- Domain architecture
- Enterprise email hub

### Phase 03: Logistic Nexus
- APOB (Additional Place of Business) registration
- FBA/FBF fulfillment center setup
- Packaging compliance

### Phase 04: Market Deployment
- Platform onboarding (Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio, Meesho)
- Brand registry
- D2C architecture
