# About TMMT

TMMT is an ecommerce infrastructure and automation company focused on marketplace operations, workflow automation, and web engineering for fashion brands and D2C businesses.

## What TMMT Builds

- Ecommerce automation systems (Python, FastAPI, OpenCV, Selenium)
- Marketplace infrastructure (Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio, Meesho)
- React / TypeScript websites and SaaS products
- Brand setup (GST, trademark, APOB, FBA/FBF)
- Performance ad systems (Meta CAPI, Google Ads, BigQuery)
- Social media content and management

## FAQ

### What does TMMT do?

TMMT builds ecommerce infrastructure — automation systems, marketplace integrations, operational dashboards, React websites, and brand systems for D2C brands.

### Who are the founders?

TMMT was co-founded by Mohit Singh (automation and systems) and Harsh Aggarwal (client strategy and UI architecture).

### What platforms does TMMT work with?

Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio, Meesho, Shopify, and custom-built ecommerce systems.

### Does TMMT work with early-stage brands?

Yes. TMMT works with brands at launch stage as well as established sellers scaling to multi-channel.

### Where is TMMT based?

Based in India, working fully remotely with brands across the country.

### What makes TMMT different from a freelancer or generic agency?

TMMT focuses exclusively on ecommerce operations. Mohit's background is automation-first; Harsh's is client-facing execution. You get both without managing multiple vendors.

### Does TMMT offer retainers or only project work?

Both. One-time builds (catalog setup, automation rollout) and monthly retainers for ongoing ops, ads management, or marketplace account health.
