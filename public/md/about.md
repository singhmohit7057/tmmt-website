# About TMMT

TMMT is a web engineering and automation studio. We build custom websites, web applications, internal tools, and workflow automation systems for businesses that want things done properly — without the overhead that usually comes with it.

## What TMMT Builds

- Custom websites and web applications (React, TypeScript, FastAPI)
- Workflow automation pipelines (Python, FastAPI, OpenCV, Selenium)
- Internal dashboards and operational tools
- Community and membership platforms
- Ecommerce marketplace infrastructure (Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio, Meesho, Shopify)
- Brand setup (GST, trademark, APOB, FBA/FBF)
- Performance ad systems (Meta CAPI, Google Ads, BigQuery)
- Social media content and management

## FAQ

### What does TMMT build?

TMMT builds custom websites, web applications, internal dashboards, and workflow automation systems. Every project is engineered from scratch — no templates, no page builders.

### What kinds of businesses does TMMT work with?

We work with businesses of all kinds — manufacturing companies, service businesses, nonprofits, startups, and growing brands. If you need a strong web presence or better-automated operations, we can help.

### Does TMMT do ecommerce?

Yes. Ecommerce is a core strength — we have deep experience with Shopify, Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio, and Meesho. Marketplace setup, inventory sync, and seller workflow automation are all part of what we do.

### How does a project with TMMT start?

You tell us what you need — a website, a tool, or a process you want automated. We scope it, agree on a plan, and build it. No lengthy onboarding, no unnecessary overhead.

### Who are the founders?

TMMT was co-founded by Mohit Singh (web engineering and automation) and Harsh Aggarwal (client strategy and UI architecture).

### Where is TMMT based?

Based in India, working fully remotely with clients across the country.

### Does TMMT offer retainers or only project work?

Both. One-time builds and monthly retainers for ongoing operations, ads management, or marketplace account health.
